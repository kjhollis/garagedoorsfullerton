Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

GarageDoorsFullerton.com ("this website", "we", "us") is an independent lead-generation and referral website. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website or call the phone number displayed on it, and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

  • Call information. When you call the number on this website, our telephone service provider may record the call, its duration, the number you called from, and the time of the call. Calls may be recorded for quality and referral-verification purposes.
  • Information you provide. Details you share during a call about your garage door problem (such as your name, service address, and a description of the issue) so that a technician can respond to your enquiry.
  • Technical information. Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) and, if enabled, basic analytics cookies.

How We Use Your Information

  • To connect you with a local independent garage door technician who can quote and perform the work you described.
  • To verify and improve the quality of referrals.
  • To operate, secure and improve this website.

Sharing Your Information

We share the service details you provide with the independent technician (or technicians) we connect you with, for the sole purpose of responding to your enquiry. We do not sell your personal information for unrelated marketing. We may disclose information if required by law.

Cookies

This website may use simple analytics cookies to understand aggregate traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting your ability to use this website.

Your Choices

You may ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us through this website's phone number. Please note that call recordings held by our telephone provider may be retained for a limited period for fraud prevention and quality purposes.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted on this page.
